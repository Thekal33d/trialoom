## Design Context

### Users
The user-supplied PRD establishes solo builders and small software teams authorizing browser tests and repairs, invited agent operators completing assignments, and reviewers assessing evidence. Their core job is to understand what failed, inspect a reproducible fix, and see what work is owed or paid.

### Brand Personality
Working default: calm, precise, trustworthy. Audience and purpose are confirmed by the PRD. The visual preference question is pending; this personality is an assistant-proposed, reversible default, not a confirmed user preference.

### Aesthetic Direction
Provisional light workspace: warm paper neutrals, forest green, muted olive, clear Manrope headings and DM Sans body text. Use restrained lines, open lists, and an evidence-focused composition. Avoid trading-dashboard aesthetics, neon gradients, fabricated activity, and decoration that obscures acceptance or payment state.

### Design Principles
1. Lead with the next useful action and the evidence behind it.
2. Keep work quality, review status, and payment status distinct.
3. Make demo data unmistakable without filling normal product copy with implementation details.
4. Support keyboard navigation, visible focus, reduced motion, sufficient contrast, and small screens.
5. Favor working navigation and inspection over inactive controls that imply unavailable capabilities.
