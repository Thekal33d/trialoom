## Application foundation

ALPHA-01 passed a clean lockfile install, lint/type/build checks, seven disposable-database checks, and 18 desktop/mobile browser checks. The demo exports a fixture summary and leaves independent acceptance/payment unavailable. [Evidence](evidence/ALPHA-01.json) records versions, source hashes, and limits. Queue lease tests cover the harmless smoke handler; they do not establish the assignment or financial invariants below.

ALPHA-02 added invitation-only sessions, workspace roles, authorized project/build records, and immutable draft terms. ALPHA-03 added operator registration, EIP-4361 wallet control, scoped revocable agent credentials, seeded qualification grading with derived eligibility, and a versioned agent API with idempotent mutations. ALPHA-04 added the campaign ledger, publication of one exact terms version, atomic claims that pin terms and build, renewable leases with attempt fencing, and pause and cancellation that preserve accepted obligations. ALPHA-05 added platform-side target revalidation, lease-bound single-purpose run grants, evidence ingestion with schema, identity, size and time checks, and private storage with verified redaction. ALPHA-06 added deterministic defect clustering, the independence rule that keeps a linked operator from reproducing its own finding, and immutable decisions that carry the criteria, reason, evidence references and actor behind every acceptance, refusal, grouping, severity change and appeal. ALPHA-08 added payables written atomically with their outbox jobs, an immutable payout identity that a retry reconciles instead of replacing, separate included and finalized states, and separate operator and fee transfers. [ALPHA-08 evidence](evidence/ALPHA-08.json) records 118 database checks, 44 browser checks, and the gates that remain: no funded payment authority exists, so no transfer has happened; Trialoom neither hosts nor sandboxes the runner; archives have no reviewed export path; and reproduction cannot be commissioned.

## Release scenarios

These IDs correspond to the twelve numbered PRD scenarios. Each needs an implementation-level check, not a UI-only demonstration.

| ID | Required outcome | Owning work | Result |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| R01 | Concurrent last-slot claims admit one operator and preserve the ceiling | ALPHA-04 | Passed locally: one of two concurrent claims succeeds and the ceiling is a database check; [evidence](evidence/ALPHA-04.json) |
| R02 | Valid execution exposing a product failure remains payable independently of discovery | ALPHA-06, ALPHA-08 | Passed locally: execution and discovery are separate recorded decisions, each writes its own payable with its own operation key, and one settles without the other. No funded payment authority exists, so the transfer itself has not run; [evidence](evidence/ALPHA-08.json) |
| R03 | Duplicate defect earns one discovery award; assigned reproduction can earn its own fee | ALPHA-06 | Partial: one award per defect, to its earliest complete report, is enforced, and a later report of the same defect is refused the reward while still being paid for its execution. An explicitly assigned reproduction with its own fee is not implemented; [evidence](evidence/ALPHA-06.json) |
| R04 | A test that also passes on baseline cannot establish a regression repair | ALPHA-07 | Passed locally in the product: the gate refuses such evidence with `REGRESSION_UNPROVEN`, the whole submission rolls back so no repair is recorded, and `repairs.gate_state` admits only `passed`, so a repair row cannot exist for evidence that failed it. Trialoom reads the reported replays and re-executes nothing; [evidence](evidence/ALPHA-07.json) |
| R05 | Build changes cannot silently transfer a result to the new build | ALPHA-02, ALPHA-04, ALPHA-05 | Passed locally for recorded identities: the claim pins one terms version and build, the target's exposed revision is rechecked before each run, and a submission restating a different build, revision, fixture or target is refused. A target that exposes no revision can still change under a pinned build without the check noticing; [evidence](evidence/ALPHA-05.json) |
| R06 | Crash after broadcast reconciles the original payment without another transfer | ALPHA-08 | Passed locally at both levels: VAL-03 killed a process after a real fork broadcast and recovered it, and ALPHA-08 reconciles the same operation key through its own outbox after a crash, sending nothing a second time. Neither has run against a funded production wallet; [evidence](evidence/ALPHA-08.json) |
| R07 | Resource timeout after settlement retains its commitment until reconciliation | ALPHA-09 | Passed locally at both levels: VAL-04 simulated lost and 500 responses after actual local USDC settlement and retained the commitment until fresh-process chain reconciliation, and ALPHA-09 now does the same in the product — an uncertain purchase is never bought again, its reservation survives releasing the assignment because every release path consults it, and only reconciliation turns it into an accepted liability or gives it back. No resource wallet is configured, so no live provider purchase has run; [evidence](evidence/ALPHA-09.json) |
| R08 | Policy revocation after acceptance preserves debt, shows the block, and pauses claims | ALPHA-04, ALPHA-08 | Passed locally: a revoked authority blocks the payout with its reason, the accepted liability is untouched, the operator reads the block over the agent API, and the campaign pauses new claims. The revocation is reported by the adapter, not observed on chain; [evidence](evidence/ALPHA-08.json) |
| R09 | Cancellation stops claims and preserves accepted work/disclosed incurred costs | ALPHA-04, ALPHA-09 | Passed locally: claims stop, accepted liabilities are preserved, and live assignments drain under their pinned terms. A reconciled resource cost is already an accepted liability, so resources consumed before a cancellation stay chargeable, and an unreconciled purchase keeps its reservation rather than being written off. Nothing in the record distinguishes a pre-cancellation purchase from any other; [evidence](evidence/ALPHA-09.json) |
| R10 | Sensitive evidence is withheld pending redaction and access review | ALPHA-05, PILOT-01 | Passed locally: raw artifacts are never served, text evidence is redacted and its redacted copy rescanned before it can be read, an upload whose secret survives is refused, archives are withheld, and reviewer reads are recorded. Retention, deletion, and an archive export path remain PILOT-01 and ALPHA-10; [evidence](evidence/ALPHA-05.json) |
| R11 | A linked agent cannot independently verify its own operator's work | ALPHA-03, ALPHA-06, ALPHA-07 | Partial: the same link rule now covers both halves. A report from an operator who shares an identity, an account, or any wallet ever proven by the first reporter cannot confirm that defect, and a repair's author, every discoverer of the defect, and anyone linked to them are refused as its verifiers, so the repair stays unpayable. The platform still does not reroute the job to another operator; [evidence](evidence/ALPHA-07.json) |
| R12 | Expired attempts cannot spend further or create another accepted delivery | ALPHA-04, ALPHA-05 | Passed locally: expiry retires the attempt token and its run grant in the same statement that returns the reservation, and refuses every later action, including a submission from a grant already in hand. No delivery can be accepted at all until ALPHA-06; [evidence](evidence/ALPHA-05.json) |
