# Current status

Last updated: 2026-10-27 (Africa/Lagos).

## State

**M2 in progress: ALPHA-08 payables, the outbox, and settlement states are verified locally.** An accepted decision now writes its payables and queues their jobs in the same transaction, so a payment never exists without its acceptance and a rolled back decision leaves neither. Each payable fixes its recipient, token, amount, decision and assignment, and carries an immutable operation key that is also its outbox dedupe key; a retry reconciles that key instead of replacing it, so a crash after broadcast recovers the original payment and sends nothing twice. Inclusion and finality are separate states, and only finality moves accepted to finalized in the ledger. Operator payouts and platform fees are separate transfers, and a failing fee never reverses a finalized payment. A lost payment authority blocks the payout with a visible reason, keeps the debt, and pauses new claims. **No funded payment authority is configured**, so the default adapter blocks every payout and no transfer has happened; Trialoom still neither hosts nor sandboxes the runner, repairs do not exist, and resource accounting is missing. M1 still has open independent-operator and paid-resource gates.
