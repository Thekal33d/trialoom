# Current status

Last updated: 2026-10-27 (Africa/Lagos).

## State

**M2: the alpha backlog is complete, ALPHA-01 to ALPHA-11, and verified locally.** `npm run loop` drives one seeded campaign from publication to an owed payable in eleven phases and thirty asserted steps: publication, two independent reports confirming a defect, decisions, four separate payables, a blocked payout that stays owed, settlement through inclusion to finality, crash-after-broadcast recovery, a fenced lapsed claim, a quoted and independently verified repair, a capped resource purchase with an uncertain one holding its reservation, the queue, and an export whose hash is verified from the file itself. It asserts as it goes and exits non-zero on the first failure, and it exercises R03, R06, R07, R11 and R12. **Three boundaries it cannot cross**: the evidence is harness-produced rather than from a hosted sandboxed runner, the payment authority is a recording fixture because none is configured, and the resource provider is a fixture because no wallet is configured. No value has moved on any network.

**ALPHA-10 is complete, so a builder can take their record out and see what is waiting.** An export of one brief is a zip holding the manifest, the findings and defects, replay instructions, the decisions with the criteria each claim pinned, the repairs and verifications, the payment ledger, and the verified redacted evidence; its content hash is taken over what the file holds and the manifest states how to recompute it. Raw bytes never leave, operator-supplied prose is redacted by the same policy, and an artifact that cannot be cleaned is named with its reason. Exporting is a builder capability and is audited. A derived queue names every pending and blocked thing across a workspace — submissions awaiting a decision and how long, ungrouped reports, flagged defects, open appeals, quotes, unverified repairs, blocked payouts, reconciliation and held reservations — with the 24-hour review target and 48-hour escalation as facts about the clock that decide nothing. **The information architecture is still panels rather than the PRD's distinct screens**, there is no operator export, and escalation is visible but not actioned.

**ALPHA-09 is complete, so a run can buy a capped resource and the cost is accounted for.** A builder's published allowance and the platform's ability to pay are separate: a resource account with its own recorded ceiling refuses what the resource wallet cannot fund, and that amount is not owed by the builder. A purchase takes a fresh quote, checks both, reserves the authorized ceiling and is recorded durably before anything is sent; a quote above the caller's explicit limit is never signed. The platform wallet pays the provider and the builder reimburses the eligible actual cost as one itemized payable the database permits only once per purchase. A purchase the platform could not deliver is absorbed and charged to nobody. An uncertain settlement is never bought again and holds its reservation through every release path until it is reconciled. **No resource wallet is configured**, the live buy adapter is not written, and nothing has been bought from a real provider.

**ALPHA-07 is complete, so the repair workflow runs end to end.** A repair is bought against one confirmed defect, one pinned build and the paths it may change, approved by the workspace owner before the brief can be published; publication is refused unless the published terms offer that same build at that same price. The quoted operator holds the first slot and everyone else verifies. A delivery that does not demonstrate a regression fix is refused outright and the whole submission rolls back, so a repair row can only exist for evidence that passed the gate. A confirmation must come from someone unconnected to the author and to every discoverer of the defect, must carry its own replays, and must exercise the same assertion the delivery was measured against. The author's work cannot be accepted until that confirmation exists, and a verifier's acceptance must state its own smaller amount. Trialoom does not merge, deploy or apply a repair, issues no repository access, and re-executes nothing.

**ALPHA-08 payables, the outbox, and settlement states are verified locally.** An accepted decision now writes its payables and queues their jobs in the same transaction, so a payment never exists without its acceptance and a rolled back decision leaves neither. Each payable fixes its recipient, token, amount, decision and assignment, and carries an immutable operation key that is also its outbox dedupe key; a retry reconciles that key instead of replacing it, so a crash after broadcast recovers the original payment and sends nothing twice. Inclusion and finality are separate states, and only finality moves accepted to finalized in the ledger. Operator payouts and platform fees are separate transfers, and a failing fee never reverses a finalized payment. A lost payment authority blocks the payout with a visible reason, keeps the debt, and pauses new claims. **No funded payment authority is configured**, so the default adapter blocks every payout and no transfer has happened; Trialoom still neither hosts nor sandboxes the runner, repairs do not exist, and resource accounting is missing. M1 still has open independent-operator and paid-resource gates.

## Next action

The two open capability gates are unchanged — VAL-02 needs an independent operator to reproduce the repair result, and VAL-04 needs one capped browser session actually provisioned, connected, traced and reconciled against a real receipt. Nothing here is evidence of mainnet behaviour or event eligibility; the hackathon checklist in [HACKATHON.md](../docs/HACKATHON.md) is separate and unmet.

Seven gaps from earlier steps belong to later work: the information architecture the PRD describes, with a service catalog, a campaign setup flow and a reviewer console as destinations rather than panels, plus an operator export and an actioned escalation path; a funded payment authority, with a real Remesso adapter, cross-campaign coordination of a shared wallet approval, and on-chain verification of a declared authorization; a funded resource wallet, with a real buy adapter, a chain read behind reconciliation, and an incident path for a purchase that stays unresolved; real runner isolation (the gVisor host, resource caps, and an egress proxy that enforces the allowlist); a reviewed export path so a reviewer can open an archived trace; commissioned reproduction, without which the PRD's assigned reproduction fee does not exist; and verification as a service of its own, since a verifier is currently paid by partial acceptance of the repair's own brief and a one-slot repair brief can never become payable.
